## Spherical Earth Quotes

**Source:** Eratosthenes, Newton, Foucault, Bradley, and modern satellite geodesy
**URL:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesy

### Measuring the Earth the old way

> "I have measured the shadow of the gnomon at Alexandria at the time of the summer solstice, and found the arc of the sun to be seven degrees and two minutes, and the shadow at Syene at the same hour to be nothing at all."

> "The distance between the two cities is about five thousand stadia, and the shadow was a little over a seventh of the circle, so the whole circumference is about seven times that."

### Shape of the Earth

> "The Earth is found to be a spheroid, flattened at the poles, and its equatorial circumference exceeds the polar by about a three hundredth part."

> "The two principal diameters of the Earth are not equal, the equatorial being about five miles longer than the polar."

### Evidence you can feel

> "A heavy body falls toward the centre of the Earth, and the direction of that fall changes with the position of the observer, which is only possible on a curved surface."

> "The pendulum plane keeps turning with the stars, turning once a day in the latitude of Paris, and the rate of the turn tells you how far you are from the pole."

### The moving heavens

> "The apparent displacement of the fixed stars, by a small quantity in the direction of the Earth's orbital motion, proves that the light is not received directly from the star."

> "An observer on the surface of the Earth travels a distance of about three hundred metres in a second of time, and so is carried sideways with respect to the incoming rays of light."

### From orbit

> "Satellite laser ranging and GPS measurements give the Earth's shape to within a few millimetres, and they show an oblate spheroid, not a sphere and certainly not a plane."

> "The Earth's equatorial radius is about six thousand three hundred and seventy-eight kilometres, and the polar radius about six thousand three hundred and fifty-six."

> "The mean radius of the Earth, taken as the average of the equatorial and polar radii, is a convenient number to put in a calculation, and it hides the fact that the Earth is not a ball at all."

### On the alternatives

> "There is no evidence for a flat Earth, and the volume and shape of the Earth have been measured directly from orbit by methods that cannot be explained away."

> "Every photograph of the Earth from space shows the same thing: a sphere, with the limb visible, and the horizon curved."
