## Biblical Verses for Flat Earth Context

**Source:** OpenBible.info - Firmament Topic
**URL:** https://www.openbible.info/topics/firmament

**Note:** Full verse extraction available in `dataset/flat-earth/biblical/scripture-verses-firmament.md`

### Key Scripture Passages

**Genesis 1:6-8** - Firmament creation
> And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.

**Job 37:18** - Skies as solid
> Hast thou with him spread out the sky, which is strong, and as a molten looking glass?

**Job 26:10** - Horizon on waters
> He hath compassed the waters with bounds, until the day and night come to an end.

**Psalm 104:5** - Earth fixed
> Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever.

**Isaiah 40:22** - Circle of earth
> It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth...

**Daniel 4:10-11** - Tree visible to ends
> I saw, and behold a tree in the midst of the earth, and the height thereof was great. The tree grew, and was strong, and the height thereof reached unto heaven, and the sight thereof to the end of all the earth.

**Matthew 4:8** - Mountain showing all kingdoms
> Again, the devil taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world...

**Revelation 7:1** - Four corners
> And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth...

### Usage Note

These verses are commonly cited in flat earth arguments. The interpretation varies between literal and metaphorical readings.
