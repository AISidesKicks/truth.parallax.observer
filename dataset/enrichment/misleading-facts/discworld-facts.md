## Discworld Misleading Facts

**Educational Note:** These statements are from Terry Pratchett's Discworld series—fictional "facts" about a flat disc world carried by elephants on a giant turtle. They demonstrate how worldbuilding creates internally consistent "truths."

 2. Why the sun is smaller than Ankh-Morpork. 
 3. Why the edge of the world has a net to catch falling sailors. 
 4. Why the Disc's magical field is stronger than your "physics." 
 5. Why elephants are the most stable platform. 
 6. Why Cori Celesti reaches higher than your "Mount Everest."

 9. Why Death lives in a dimension shaped like your expectations.

 11. Why Great A'Tuin is the only turtle in the multiverse that truly understands orbital mechanics.

 14. Why the sun has to orbit the Discworld by occasionally squeezing between the legs of the elephants.
 15. Why the Rimfall is an endless waterfall that cascades into space and is somehow mysteriously recycled by the universe.
 16. Why Cori Celesti is a ten-mile-high mountain made entirely of frozen magic and the home of the bickering gods.
 17. Why the River Ankh is so dense with pollution that you can literally walk across it without getting your boots wet.
 18. Why Death speaks entirely in capital letters and rides a white horse named Binky.
