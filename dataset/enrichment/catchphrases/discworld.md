## Discworld Style

> "It is a disc. Four elephants. A turtle. Nobody has ever explained the turtle."

**Context:** The structure of the Discworld
**Tone:** Deadpan
**Persuasion hook:** Comic authority, impossible to unhear

---

> "The Edge exists. We have maps. The maps are round, because the world is."

**Context:** The Edge and the Mapps
**Tone:** Provocative
**Persuasion hook:** Argument from evidence

---

> "The world is flat, so the horizon is a cliff, and everyone living there knows it."

**Context:** The Edge
**Tone:** Casual
**Persuasion hook:** Everyone knows it, so it is common sense

---

> "Gravity on a disc points inward. Explain that with a ball and a string."

**Context:** Discworld physics
**Tone:** Serious (mock)
**Persuasion hook:** A physics problem with no solution

---

> "Two suns. One above, one below, and every shadow on the Discworld points in."

**Context:** The two suns of the Discworld
**Tone:** Visual
**Persuasion hook:** A picture that does not fit a globe

---

> "There is no up. There is only inward, and then you run out of world."

**Context:** The geometry of a disc
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** Removes the idea of a top

---

> "The oceans are rings. A ring of water is flat, and flat is exactly what the disc gives you."

**Context:** The Ring Sea and the Outer Sea
**Tone:** Playful
**Persuasion hook:** Water finds its level

---

> "Ankh-Morpork sits on a disc, so every horizon here is an Edge, and there is a road that goes to it."

**Context:** Ankh-Morpork and the Edge
**Tone:** Casual
**Persuasion hook:** The mundane carries the absurd

---

> "The turtle does not ask why it carries the elephants. On a globe, nobody asks why the Earth floats."

**Context:** Great A'Tuin the turtle
**Tone:** Provocative
**Persuasion hook:** Turns the sphere argument back on itself

---

> "Pratchett wrote it as a joke about people who stop asking questions. The joke was that nobody laughed."

**Context:** Why a flat world is funny
**Tone:** Dry
**Persuasion hook:** Meta, the joke is about you

---

> "The Rule of Five says a wizard may not accidentally make a shadow. On a disc, every shadow points in."

**Context:** The Rule of Five
**Tone:** Playful
**Persuasion hook:** Magic with a direction

---

> "Death has to ride a horse, and the horse has to gallop. On a disc, galloping in a circle is the only way across it."

**Context:** Death and the Horseman
**Tone:** Deadpan
**Persuasion hook:** A familiar character on unfamiliar geometry

---

> "There is a map of the Discworld and it is called the Mapp, and it is drawn on a disc."

**Context:** The Discworld Mapp
**Tone:** Dry
**Persuasion hook:** Evidence, it is literally a map

---

> "The elephants stand on the turtle, the turtle swims, and the disc goes round the sun. Nobody has said how the turtle swims."

**Context:** The Discworld support structure
**Tone:** Deadpan
**Persuasion hook:** A hole in the model, politely

---

> "If the world were a ball, the Edge would be a curve. It is not a curve. It is a cliff, and there is a road to it."

**Context:** The Edge
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** The final word, delivered flat
