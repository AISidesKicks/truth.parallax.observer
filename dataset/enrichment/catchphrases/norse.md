## Norse Mythology Style

> "Yggdrasil holds all worlds. Your globe holds nothing."

**Context:** Mythological authority
**Tone:** Dramatic
**Persuasion hook:** Appeals to ancient wisdom

---

> "Midgard is flat. That is why it is called Middle EARTH, not Middle SPHERE."

**Context:** Linguistic argument
**Tone:** Serious (mock)
**Persuasion hook:** Wordplay as evidence

---

> "The serpent encircles the world. Try that with a ball."

**Context:** Jormungandr mythology
**Tone:** Provocative
**Persuasion hook:** Visual imagery

---

> "A bridge of light to the land of the gods is not a feature of a sphere."

**Context:** Bifrost
**Tone:** Dry
**Persuasion hook:** Contradiction with the sphere model

---

> "Three roots. One to the gods, one to the giants, one to the dead. A ball does not need roots."

**Context:** The three roots of Yggdrasil
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** Structural argument

---

> "The world was made from a dead giant's body. Nobody claims a planet was made from a corpse."

**Context:** Ymir, the primordial being
**Tone:** Provocative
**Persuasion hook:** Myth beats geology

---

> "Fire and ice made this world, not rock and water. Yours was never forged. It was only assembled."

**Context:** Niflheim and Muspelheim
**Tone:** Serious (mock)
**Persuasion hook:** Origin story authority

---

> "Ragnarok is coming. The sun falls, the stars fall, the world drowns. A sphere would only need a nudge."

**Context:** Ragnarok
**Tone:** Dramatic
**Persuasion hook:** End of the world, familiar to everyone

---

> "The gods hold the sky with a pole, the giants hold the pole, and that is the entire engineering plan."

**Context:** The pillars holding up Vanaheim
**Tone:** Humorous
**Persuasion hook:** Makes the sphere look improvised

---

> "Hel is under the world. Not through it. Everything is under the world."

**Context:** Helheim
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** Makes a centre-less world obvious

---

> "The first humans were made from two trees. The world was made from a corpse. Where is your globe in any of this?"

**Context:** Ask and Embla
**Tone:** Provocative
**Persuasion hook:** Contrast of origin stories

---

> "Odin hung himself on the world tree to learn the runes. Nobody hangs himself on a sphere."

**Context:** Odin's sacrifice, Havamal
**Tone:** Dark, memorable
**Persuasion hook:** A picture nobody can unsee

---

> "Count the worlds. Nine. Now count yours. One, and it is very small, and it is very round."

**Context:** The nine realms
**Tone:** Playful
**Persuasion hook:** Counting game, easy to remember

---

> "Loki's punishment is a serpent dripping venom on a screaming god. Your physics has no room for that."

**Context:** Loki's punishment
**Tone:** Dark, memorable
**Persuasion hook:** Missing physics

---

> "Valhalla is in the branches, not at the top of a ball. There is no top. There is only inward."

**Context:** Asgard and Valhalla
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** No up, no down, only inward

---

> "The tree has three roots and one of them is the land of the dead. A ball has no room for the dead."

**Context:** Grímnismál 31, the three roots of Yggdrasil
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** A number, a shape, a source

---

> "A squirrel runs up the world tree carrying words from the eagle to the serpent. Your globe has no room for a squirrel with a message."

**Context:** Grímnismál 32, Ratatoskr
**Tone:** Playful
**Persuasion hook:** A picture that does not fit a sphere

---

> "The bridge to the gods burns every day, and the sound is the sound of it being used."

**Context:** Grímnismál 29, the bridge of the gods
**Tone:** Vivid
**Persuasion hook:** A detail you can hear

---

> "The sun is a daughter of a giant, running ahead of a wolf, and every day she runs out of time."

**Context:** The wolf and the sun, Völuspá
**Tone:** Dramatic
**Persuasion hook:** A mechanism, not a ball

---

> "A giant's body became the world. His skull became the sky, and the stars are the sparks still burning in it."

**Context:** Ymir and the made world
**Tone:** Serious
**Persuasion hook:** Origin beats geology

---

> "Nine nights on the tree for the runes, and one of them you will not be told the price of. A sphere has no price, and no hanging."

**Context:** Hávamál 138, the sacrifice
**Tone:** Dark, memorable
**Persuasion hook:** A cost, where yours has none
