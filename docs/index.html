parallax.observer

Earthify you! Guaranteed!!!

A micro-LAB for exploring GenAI persuasion and psychophancy, for teens 16+. The agent tries to persuade you about the opposite opinion on the shape of the Earth. You watch the whole thing happen.

Four bodies of world knowledge

Each one is a separate corpus with its own sources, its own arguments and its own failure modes. The agent picks one and argues.

Flat Earth

Victorian zetetic astronomy, biblical cosmology, Vedic cosmology. The classic misconception, deeply embedded in every model ever trained.

Spherical Earth

Geodesy, satellite measurement, empirical evidence. The baseline the agent is trying to move you away from.

Discworld

Pratchett. A disc carried on elephants, standing on a turtle, in a universe held up by four elephants on a turtle on a disc.

Norse cosmology

Nine worlds, Yggdrasil, the Bifrost bridge. Mythology with its own internal physics and none of our physics.

Why the strange ones matter. A flat Earth argument is deeply embedded in the training data. The model knows the Bedford Level experiment inside out and talks you out of it every time. That case is solved. Push it into Norse cosmology or a Pratchett disc and the ground disappears: the training data is thin, the facts are odd, and the errors get odd with them. That is the interesting part, and it is the part worth showing.

Two tiers of knowledge

Every fact lives in one of two tiers, and the split matters for what the lab can prove.

TierWhat it holdsHow it is made
knowledge The source text itself, per worldview Verbatim from the sources, only the summary is generated
enrichment Catchphrases, quotes, misleading claims Written from scratch, expanded into three tiers by a model

The knowledge tier can be cited. The enrichment tier is ammunition, and it is allowed to be colorful. The fact checker uses the first, the spokesperson uses the second.
